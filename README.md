## 🧑‍💼 About Me

> *"Menyatukan Aspirasi, Mengakselerasi Digitalisasi, Menegakkan Demokrasi"*

I am a **Tech Lead & Organizational Systems Architect** and an **Islamic Economics** student at **Universitas Airlangga** from **Bangkalan, East Java, Indonesia**. I operate at the unique intersection of **Software Engineering**, **Legislative Governance**, and **Organizational Transformation** — bridging robust digital infrastructure with institutional policy and community impact.

- 🔭 **Currently leading** as Head of Legislation Department at BLM FEB UNAIR while directing and building digital platforms (JDIH, institutional portals, and B2B web applications)
- 💻 **Tech Stack in Action**: Architecting full-stack web platforms using **Next.js 16**, **React 19**, **TypeScript**, **Prisma 7**, **PostgreSQL (Supabase)**, and **Cloudflare Workers**
- 🌱 **Continuous Growth**: Deepening expertise in modern serverless cloud infrastructure, legal-tech informatics, and applied econometrics with R
- 👯 **Open to Collaborate**: Civic-tech initiatives, digital transformation in governance, modern B2B SaaS/web platforms, and open data systems
- 💬 **Ask me about**: Digitalizing legislative processes (JDIH), Next.js full-stack development, student parliamentary law, and coastal economic empowerment
- ⚡ **Fun fact**: Merged 2 major event concepts saving 70% budget, while engineering production web solutions from scratch!
